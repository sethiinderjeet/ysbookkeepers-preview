FAQ

Questions from other health practitioners

I don't see my profession listed. Can you still help?
Almost certainly. If you work in health and wellness in BC, we understand how your practice earns and gets taxed. Book a call and tell us about it.
Do you work with non-regulated wellness professionals?
Yes. Yoga and Pilates instructors, personal trainers and holistic practitioners have their own GST and expense considerations, and we work with them too.
Are you virtual?
Yes. We work with practitioners across BC using secure cloud tools, so location is never a barrier.
