FAQ's

Pricing questions

Which plan is right for me?
If you’re incorporated and keep your own books, start with Solo Practice/Essential. Once you have staff or want us to handle your bookkeeping, move to Growth. If you run a clinic with associates or a business that needs regular reporting and planning, choose Group Practice/Scale. Not sure? We’ll recommend one on your call.
Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can move to a different plan as your business needs change, with 30 days' notice.
Is there a contract or setup fee?
Our plans are month-to-month, with no long-term commitment or standard setup fee. If your bookkeeping needs cleanup, we'll review the work and provide a separate quote before starting.
Is my personal tax return included?
Personal tax returns are priced separately. Monthly-plan clients receive preferred T1 pricing starting at $199.
Do I need to be located in BC?
No. We're based in Delta, BC, and work virtually with clients across Canada, except Quebec. Most of our work is handled through our secure client portal, email and video calls. We're also happy to arrange in-person meetings with clients in Vancouver and the Lower Mainland.
I’m behind on my books or switching accountants. Can you still help?
Absolutely. We'll review your records, identify any outstanding work and provide a quote before getting started. If you're switching accountants, we'll guide you through the transition.
