FAQ

Questions About Corporate Tax

Tax information last reviewed: October 2026

When is my corporate tax return due?
Your T2 return is generally due six months after your corporation's fiscal year-end. Any tax owing is usually due within two months, or three months for qualifying Canadian-controlled private corporations (CCPCs). We help you understand both deadlines and plan ahead.
Should I pay myself salary or dividends?
It depends on your income needs, your personal tax bracket and your long-term plans. Salary creates RRSP room and CPP contributions, while dividends are simpler to pay and don’t require payroll. Many owners use a mix. We help you compare the options before your year-end.
Can I pay personal expenses through my corporation?
Personal expenses paid by your corporation need to be recorded and treated correctly. They may result in a shareholder loan or taxable shareholder benefit. Certain shareholder loans can become taxable if not repaid within the required period. We help you review your balances and understand the tax implications.
Does my corporation need to pay tax instalments?
Generally, yes, if your corporation’s tax was more than $3,000 in both the current and previous year. New corporations don’t pay instalments in their first year. We let you know what applies to you.
Can you prepare my T2 if someone else handles my bookkeeping?
Yes. We can prepare your corporate tax return using financial records maintained by you or another bookkeeper, provided they're complete and suitable for tax filing. If adjustments or cleanup are needed, we'll explain the additional work.
